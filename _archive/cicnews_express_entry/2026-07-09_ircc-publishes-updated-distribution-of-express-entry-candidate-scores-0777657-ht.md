## Which draws have not been accounted for in the July 5 distribution?
The July 5 distribution does not consider the two most recent [Express Entry draws](https://www.canadavisa.com/express-entry-invitations-to-apply-issued.html?utm_source=cicnews.com&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=2026-07-07_ircc-publishes-updated-distribution-of-express-entry-candidate-scores_77657) conducted by [Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada](https://www.canadavisa.com/ircc.html?utm_source=cicnews.com&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=2026-07-07_ircc-publishes-updated-distribution-of-express-entry-candidate-scores_77657) (IRCC) on July 6 and July 7, through which a combined total of 2,534 invitations were issued:
Date of draw| Round type| Invitations issued| CRS score of lowest-ranked candidate invited  
---|---|---|---  
July 6| Provincial Nominee Program| 534| 708  
July 7| Canadian Experience Class| 2,000| 517  
## What accounted for the changes in Express Entry profiles between June 21 and July 5?
IRCC conducted four draws in the two-week span between June 21 and July 5, cumulatively issuing 9,226 invitations.
Date of draw| Round type| Invitations issued| CRS score of lowest-ranked candidate invited  
---|---|---|---  
June 22| Provincial Nominee Program| 955| 730  
June 23| Canadian Experience Class| 4,000| 516  
June 24| Physicians with Canadian Work Experience| 271| 223  
June 25| Healthcare and Social Services Occupations| 4,000| 475  
Over this period, the overall size of the Express Entry pool declined by 4,518 profiles.
## Permanent residence selection through Express Entry
Express Entry is the federal government’s flagship application management system for the selection of permanent residents intending to settle in Canada outside Quebec.
The government periodically issues [invitations to apply](https://www.canadavisa.com/express-entry-invitation-to-apply-for-permanent-residence.html?utm_source=cicnews.com&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=2026-07-07_ircc-publishes-updated-distribution-of-express-entry-candidate-scores_77657) for [permanent residence](https://www.canadavisa.com/canadian-immigration-residency-obligations.html?utm_source=cicnews.com&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=2026-07-07_ircc-publishes-updated-distribution-of-express-entry-candidate-scores_77657) to top-scoring candidates with active profiles in the Express Entry system.
To enter the Express Entry pool, candidates must first meet the minimum requirements for one of the system’s programs. Their profile receives a CRS score based on factors including age, education, official language ability, and work experience.
The government also holds draws under [category-based selection](https://www.canadavisa.com/eecategories.html?utm_source=cicnews.com&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=2026-07-07_ircc-publishes-updated-distribution-of-express-entry-candidate-scores_77657), in which it issues invitations only to candidates meeting the requirements for an identified priority economic category.
The current priority categories focus on French-language proficiency and work experience in select occupations tied to in-demand sectors, including healthcare and social services, trades, education, and transportation.
