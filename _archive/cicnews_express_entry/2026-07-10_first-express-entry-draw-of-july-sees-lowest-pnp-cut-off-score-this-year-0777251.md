Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) has issued more invitations to apply (ITA) through the Express Entry system.
The immigration department issued 534 ITAs in a draw for Provincial Nominee Program candidates.
To be considered in this draw, candidates needed to have:
  * A minimum Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score of 708; and
  * Created an Express Entry candidate profile before 2:49 p.m., Universal Coordinated Time (UTC) on June 4, 2026.
