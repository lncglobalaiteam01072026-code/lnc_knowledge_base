Are you an entrepreneur looking to buy a business in Canada? Wondering how to make it happen from abroad? The story of ‘Aziz’ (not his real name), a determined business owner from Uzbekistan, may inspire you to take action. With careful planning and the proper support, Aziz was able to buy and transform a beloved local café in British Columbia. Here’s how he did it, the challenges he faced, and what made his journey successful.
![A bearded barista in a brown apron smiles while holding a tablet, perhaps reviewing a business plan template, in a cozy coffee shop as two coworkers prepare drinks in the background.](https://sobirovs.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/BC-Local-cafe-1024x576.jpg)
## **Background Information**
Aziz’s aimed to buy an existing company in BC for $100,000 and use the remaining capital to revitalize it with new ideas and investments. Here’s a snapshot of his background:
  * **Citizenship:** Uzbekistan
  * **Industry:** Café and hospitality
  * **Proposed Investment Amount:** CAD $75,000 to buy the business, plus an additional CAD $125,000 for improvements
  * **Business Vision:** To expand and enhance the café’s offerings while preserving its role as a cornerstone of the local community
  * **Language Ability:** Fluent in English, with an IELTS score that exceeded program requirements
  * **Past Experience:** Over 10 years in senior managerial roles, including experience running retail businesses
  * **Education:** Bachelor of Laws and a certificate in Coffee House Management
  * **Age:** 47 years old


## **Issues and Challenges**
Buying a business in Canada from abroad is complex, and Aziz faced several key challenges:
  1. **Finding the Right Business:** Purchasing an existing business involves careful due diligence, negotiations, and meeting the IRCC’s significant benefit requirements. We helped Aziz search for a suitable business that matched his budget, performed background checks on the café, and negotiated a fair deal with the seller.
  2. **Inability to Visit Canada:** Without a Temporary Resident Visa, Aziz could not visit Canada to conduct an in-person interview with the seller or take another exploratory trip. Instead, we arranged an online exploratory trip, where he met with local stakeholders and even secured a letter of support from the town’s mayor.
  3. **Conditional Purchase:** Since Aziz couldn’t complete the business purchase without first securing his work permit, we worked closely with the seller to hold the business until his permit was approved.


## **Immigration Program: C11 LMIA-Exempt Work Permit**
The C11 program is designed for entrepreneurs who can demonstrate that their business will bring significant benefits to Canada. It enabled Aziz to secure a work permit without requiring a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA). Here’s how we supported him:
  * **Steps Taken:**
    * **Business Search Assistance:** We supported Aziz in finding the right business by conducting a thorough search to identify options that would fit his budget and meet the C11 program’s requirements for significant benefits to Canada. Our team performed due diligence on various businesses, ultimately helping him select a viable café with strong community ties and growth potential. This ensured his investment aligned with his business goals and the program’s criteria.
    * **Company Registration:** Helped Aziz incorporate his company in Canada to facilitate the business purchase.
    * **Crystallizing Business Vision** : Collaborated to refine his business plan, focusing on revitalizing the café with new offerings and community events.
    * **Demonstrating Significant Benefits** : Provided evidence such as the letter from the mayor, plans to maintain existing jobs, and revitalization efforts to benefit the local economy.
    * **Application Preparation** : Prepared a comprehensive application that clearly demonstrated how Aziz’s experience and investment would contribute to the community.


## **Application Outcome: Approved in 4 Months**
Aziz’s work permit was approved within four months. Several factors contributed to the success of Aziz’s application:
  1. **Strong Evidence of Significant Benefits:** We provided robust evidence, including a letter of support from the town’s mayor, showing how Aziz’s investment would bring benefits to the area economically.
  2. **Commitment to Buy an Existing Business:** Aziz’s intention to purchase a well-loved local business allowed the retiring owner to exit with capital while ensuring continuity for the community.
  3. **Solid Business Plan and Sufficient Capital:** Aziz’s plan to improve the café and invest in new services was backed by substantial financial resources, showing his readiness to execute the vision.
  4. **Client’s Dedication:** Aziz was proactive in his approach, participating in online exploratory trips, meeting with stakeholders, and collaborating with us throughout the application process. His positive attitude and flexibility were instrumental in the case’s success.
