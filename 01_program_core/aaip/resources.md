## Introduction
Below are tools and resources to help you complete an Expression of Interest, and if you are selected, how to apply to the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP). On the webinar page, you can register for upcoming webinars and watch recorded webinars.
## Check your eligibility for AAIP
Before you begin, make sure you are eligible to apply for AAIP and what stream you should apply for.
[Use the AAIP Eligibility Explorer](https://aaip.alberta.ca/eligibility-explorer)
## AAIP common questions
Check out our [answers to common questions](https://www.alberta.ca/aaip-answers-for-common-questions "AAIP answers for common questions") asked about the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program.
## Webinars
Live and recorded webinars and videos are available to help you learn more about AAIP streams and pathways.
  * [Register for new AAIP webinars or watch recorded webinars and videos](https://www.alberta.ca/alberta-advantage-immigration-program-webinars "Alberta Advantage Immigration Program – Webinars")


## National Occupational Classification
The National Occupational Classification (NOC) system, developed by the Government of Canada, classifies every job in Canada and assigns it a 5-digit National Occupational Classification code.
  * Your NOC code will determine your eligibility to apply to AAIP.


Use the Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) [Find your NOC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/eligibility/find-national-occupation-code.html) tool to determine the NOC code that best fits your occupation.
## IQAS
The [International Qualifications Assessment Service](https://www.alberta.ca/international-qualifications-assessment "International Qualifications Assessment Service \(IQAS\)") (IQAS) will assess your foreign educational credentials and provide information on how your credentials compare to educational standards in Canada.
## Translation
AAIP accepts educational documents, work reference letters or other documents in English or French. Documents in other languages may require translation. Details on translation requirements are included in AAIP Document Checklists for each stream.
