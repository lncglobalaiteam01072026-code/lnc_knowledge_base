### With a rich history spanning more than 150 years, we have produced thousands of alumni.
We’re proud of our people, and that doesn’t end when they move on from Lane Neave.
Our alumni network is about developing a lifelong relationship with our former staff, lawyers and Partners. Being connected with the Lane Neave Alumni group will help you grow your own networks, as well as keeping in touch with all the latest news, developments and events at Lane Neave.
