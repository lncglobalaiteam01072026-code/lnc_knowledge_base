# About us
Queen City Law is a multi-award-winning law firm including winning the top award for Property & Construction lawyers in New Zealand five times and NZ’s number one Boutique law firm three times. These things don’t happen by chance. Rather it reflects a law firm at the top of its game, playing to its respective strengths and living a culture that creates excellence in its personnel and the firm’s legal output. We are a client-centric team that clients love working with, and we enjoy assisting clients reach their goals and always try and focus on adding bottom line value. Feel free to reach out and see if we can become lawyers and trusted advisors that you can rely on.
— Marcus Beveridge | Managing Director

Legal Confidence, Business Instinct
Queen City Law was founded in 2004. Since its inception, the business has achieved continuous growth and success. Most notably, we have been a multiple recipient for the much-coveted New Zealand Boutique Law Firm of the Year, and the New Zealand Property and Construction Law firm of the year awards.
Our firm has earned respect through its commitment to deliver excellence. Our core belief is centered around the importance of client satisfaction and delivering value.
By placing our clients' interests at the core of our practice, the results of our success is through delivering innovative solutions for our clients. That’s the real reward.

## Our values
At Queen City Law we are committed to providing our clients with the highest quality of legal services. We believe that our values reflect who we are, and how we work. 
###### Client-Centricity
Our clients are the core of our business. We strive to provide a positive experience and build long lasting relationships. We are responsive, accessible and transparent in our communication. 
###### Collaboration
We leverage the diversity and expertise of our team to deliver exceptional outcomes for our clients. We collaborate with our clients, colleagues and external partners to achieve the best results. 
###### Agility 
Our firm provides agile and tailored solutions that align with our clients changing circumstances. We embrace change and are open to new ideas, learning from experience and delivering value to our clients. 
###### Excellence
We aim for excellence in everything we do. We recruit, train, and retain the best talent in the industry. We use our expertise, creativity, and innovation to solve complex legal challenges. We uphold the highest standards of professionalism and ethics.
###### Integrity
We act with honesty, fairness and respect in all of our dealings. We foster a culture of trust and loyalty with our clients, and within the firm. 
###### Solutions
We use critical thinking and collaborative skills to find the best possible solutions for our clients.
