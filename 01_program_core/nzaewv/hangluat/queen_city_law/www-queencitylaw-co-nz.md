## Focused on providing solutions
We are a client-centric, passionate team of experienced professionals. Putting a strong emphasis on adding value for our clients, while empowering them to navigate complex legal landscapes with confidence and peace of mind. 
Our legal team is well connected with a wide range of business contacts and you can trust that we will deliver the results you desire.
Queen City Law specialises in Commercial Law, Immigration Law, Litigation and Property Development.
We are a multilingual firm, with staff who speak Mandarin, Cantonese, Korean, Tagalog, Indonesian, Russian, Spanish and French.

## Areas of expertise
### Banking and Finance
Our team offers legal advice for financial transactions, ensuring compliance and security for lenders and borrowers.
### Commercial
Our expertise supports businesses in legal matters ranging from corporate structuring to commercial agreements.
### Construction
We provide legal counsel on construction contracts, regulatory compliance, and dispute resolution.
### Employment
Our services support both employers and employees in navigating the legal framework surrounding the workplace.
### Immigration
We navigate the complexities of immigration law, assisting clients with visa applications and residency matters.
### Litigation
We represent clients in legal disputes, aiming for the best outcome while mitigating risk.
### Real Estate
We provide tailored legal services for high-value commercial real estate and land development projects. 
### Property
We handle all residential property matters, and we guide clients through the legal aspects of property development projects, from land acquisition to construction and sales. Our firm also offers legal expertise in commercial property transactions and leasing, ensuring your investments are legally sound.
### Private Wealth
We craft strategies to protect your assets and plan for the future, from trusts to succession planning.
