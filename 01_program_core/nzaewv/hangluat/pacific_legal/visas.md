  * NZ Permanent Residency Visas
  * Skilled Migrant Visas and Business Visas
  * Temporary Visas (Work, Student, Visitor)
  * Pacific Access and Samoan Quota
  * Temporary Visas as exceptions to policy
  * Section 61 Requests to be placed on lawful visas by unlawful people
  * Deportation Orders and Detention issues
  * All NZ Residency appeals
  * Complaints and reviews for recently declined temporary applications
  * Religious Worker category work visas
  * Employer assistance (skilled migrants)
  * NZ Citizenship


### No two cases are the same
We know that no individual is the same and that clients require solutions and strategies that are tailored to their particular circumstances.
You will benefit from our experience in providing innovative solutions and strategies to meet your needs from the start to the end of the NZ immigration process.
Our services are designed to ensure that all our clients are kept up to date with the progress of their application. 
**We are focused on minimising delays and obtaining the best result for your case.**
### Policies & Case Support
Rules around health, character, identity and other aspects are being tightened. Immigration policies change constantly. A simple straightforward case can become difficult through even the smallest changes of policy. If you are doing your own case and find yourself in this situation, you may have only have a short period to respond. We can help avoid this situation through our prior knowledge of other similar cases and strong working relationship with Immigration New Zealand. However if problems still occur, we can put in a quick and competent response. 
Through our regular in-house training and mentoring processes our team is kept constantly up to date. We use state of the art practice management systems to manage cases and watch deadlines.
### Immigration Support available to you wherever you are in NZ or the world.
**We have clients throughout the Asia Pacific region, Canada, South America, the United States, France and beyond, as well as here in New Zealand.**
Our immigration lawyers are available New Zealand wide and we frequently visit Christchurch, Hamilton and many other centres throughout NZ. We have one permanent office in Auckland and we are also available to you via email and Skype.
