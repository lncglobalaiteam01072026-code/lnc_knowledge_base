# Get your documents ready

You may need information from some or all of these documents when you create a profile:

- a passport or travel document
  - If your passport expires **within 6 months** from the day you apply, you should renew it. When you get your new passport, [update your passport information](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=1138&top=29) in your application.
  - **Venezuelan passport holders**: You may need to [complete extra steps](#centred-popup1) when you create a profile or fill out your application.
- [language test results](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-test.html)
- proof of Canadian education or an [educational credential assessment report](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/education-assessment.html) for immigration purposes if:
  - you’re applying through the Federal Skilled Workers Program, **or**
  - you want to get points for the education you got outside Canada
- [written job offer from an employer in Canada](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/job-offer.html) (if you have one)
- proof of work experience (such as a reference or experience letter from an employer)
- certificate of qualification in a trade occupation issued by a Canadian province or territory (if you have one)
- [proof of funds](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/proof-funds.html)
- [police certificates](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/police-certificates.html)
- [provincial nomination](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/provincial-nominees/express-entry/get-confirm-nomination.html) (if you applied for the Provincial Nominee Program)

## If the printed expiry date on your Venezuelan passport has passed

Follow these steps, if the printed expiry date has passed, and you’re creating a profile or submitting an application under Express Entry:

1. Add 5 years to the printed expiry date shown on your passport and enter it in the expiry date field when creating your profile or submitting your online application.
2. Include a letter of explanation with your application stating: “I am a Venezuelan national with a Venezuelan passport, which has been extended for five years.”

**Warning:** If your passport is still expired after adding 5 years to the printed expiry date, you cannot use it to create a profile or submit an application through Express Entry.
