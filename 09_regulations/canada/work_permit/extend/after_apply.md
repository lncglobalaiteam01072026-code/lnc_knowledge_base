# After you extend

## If you applied online to extend or change your work permit

After you apply online, we’ll send you a letter in your account (WP-EXT except PGWP) that you can use as proof that you’re authorized to keep working, [if you’re eligible](#centred-popup1).

- You can print this letter or show it with your current work permit.
- The letter is proof that you’re authorized to keep working under the **same conditions as your original work permit** until we process your application.
  - You can keep working past the **expiry date on the letter** until we process your application if the conditions are the same as on your original work permit.

### Some applicants won’t receive a proof of authorized work letter

You’re not eligible to receive a letter (WP-EXT except PGWP) that proves you’re authorized to work if you

- applied for an extension to your work permit after your work permit expired
- applied for your first work permit in Canada

### Why there’s an expiry date on the letter

Your letter automatically lists an expiry date that’s 365 days from the date we receive your application. The expiry date will help satisfy concerns from your employer and health insurance providers about your status and authorization to work in Canada.

### If we don’t finalize your application by the date on your letter

If we don’t finalize your application by the date on your letter, **you can still keep working until we make a decision**.

If your employer or others need proof that you can continue to work, you can show them this web page.

[Learn more about working while we process your application](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/extend/expired-permit.html).

## If you applied on paper to extend or change your work permit

If you applied on paper, you won’t get a letter (WP-EXT except PGWP) that proves you’re authorized to work. This is because we need to make sure that your application is complete and that you meet the requirements to work.

If you applied to extend or change your work permit before it expired, you’re authorized to continue working under the **same conditions as your current work permit**.

You can show your employer proof that you submitted your application. Valid forms of proof include

- a copy of your work permit application
- proof that you paid your fees
- a copy of postal or courier tracking information

**Temporary resident permit applicants do not benefit from maintained status**

Temporary resident permit (TRP) applicants **can’t maintain temporary resident status while their application is being processed**. This applies to both

- initial TRP applications, and
- any other TRP applications

## What happens if we approve your application

You’ll get

- an extension of your current work permit, or
- a work permit with new conditions on it, including
  - the type of work you can do
  - the employer you can work for
  - where you can work

### Working while you wait for your work permit to arrive

We’ll send you a note in your online account letting you know your work permit has been approved. You can start working as soon as you receive this note.

You don’t have to wait until you get your work permit in the mail to start working.
