# Hire a temporary foreign worker with a Labour Market Impact Assessment

Determine which type of Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) application you should submit to hire a temporary foreign worker. Understand your responsibilities when hiring under the Temporary Foreign Worker Program.

## Information about possible wildfires and floods

If you are affected by wildfires or floods, visit our [Help for individuals affected by hazardous weather](/en/employment-social-development/corporate/notices/hazardous-weather.html) page for more information on how Service Canada can support.

## Ebola disease: Temporary measures

For employers with workers arriving from the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC), Uganda and South Sudan, consult [Ebola disease: Temporary measures](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/special-measures/ebola-2026.html) to learn to more on the Government of Canada’s temporary measures in response to the Ebola disease.

## Changes to the Temporary Foreign Worker Program for low-wage positions

Recent changes have been made to the Temporary Foreign Worker Program:

- As of April 1, 2026, employers submitting a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) application for low-wage positions must:
  - [advertise the job offer for a minimum of 8 consecutive weeks](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/median-wage/low/requirements.html#duration) in the 3 months before submitting the application, and
  - [target youth](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/median-wage/low/requirements.html#youth) in recruitment efforts
- As early as April 1, 2026, employers in rural areas [may be eligible for temporary measures](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/temporary-measures.html) on the proportion of temporary foreign workers hired for some low-wage positions located in regions outside census metropolitan areas and within participating provinces and territories

## Determine what stream to apply for

### [High and low-wage positions](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/median-wage.html)

Hire a temporary foreign worker for a wide range of positions based on the provincial or territorial wage threshold.

### [Primary agriculture positions](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/agricultural.html)

Hire an agricultural temporary foreign worker in occupations and activities related to primary agriculture.

### [Applications to support permanent residency](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/permanent.html)

Hire a skilled temporary foreign worker to support their permanent residence through an immigration program.

### [Global Talent Stream](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/global-talent.html)

Hire uniquely skilled talent or in-demand workers to scale up and grow your business.

### [Caregiver positions](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/caregiver.html)

Hire a temporary foreign worker to care for children, seniors or persons with medical needs.

### [Foreign academic positions](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/academic.html)

Hire a foreign academic at a degree-granting post-secondary educational institution in Canada.

### [Hiring in the province of Quebec](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/quebec.html)

Hire temporary foreign workers for positions in Quebec via regular or facilitated processes.

### [Recognized Employer Pilot](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/recognized-employer.html)

Hire temporary foreign workers through a simplified online application process.
