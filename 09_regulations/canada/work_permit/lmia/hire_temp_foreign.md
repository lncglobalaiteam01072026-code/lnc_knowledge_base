# Hire a temporary foreign worker

Attract and hire global talent with or without an LMIA (labour market impact assessment).

![](/content/dam/ircc/images/topic-pages/temp-worker.jpg)

**Work permit extension for workers in Quebec**

Starting March 13, 2026, your worker may be eligible for a labour market impact assessment (LMIA) exemption.

[Find out more about this exemption](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/hire-temporary-foreign/extend-permit/quebec-exemption.html).
