# Hire a home care worker (caregiver)

There are a few ways you can hire a home care worker (caregiver). Your options depend on

- whether you’re inside or outside Quebec
- where the home care worker (caregiver) will work

The information on this page applies to employers looking to hire a

- **home child care provider** ([National Occupation Classification 44100](https://noc.esdc.gc.ca/Structure/NOCProfile?code=44100&version=2021.0))
- **home support worker** ([National Occupation Classification 44101](https://noc.esdc.gc.ca/Structure/NOCProfile?code=44101&version=2021.0))

If you need to hire a worker in **another occupation**, [follow the process to hire a foreign worker](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada.html#employers).

## Hire a worker who’s in Canada

If you’re an employer (outside Quebec), you can hire a home care worker through one of the programs below.

### Home Care Worker Immigration Pilots: Workers in Canada stream: Option 1 of 4

Closed

The [Home Care Worker Immigration pilots](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/caregivers/home-care-worker-immigration-pilots/child-care-home-support.html): Workers in Canada stream allows Canadian employers (outside Quebec) to hire qualified home care workers already in Canada.

[How to hire under this pilot](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/caregivers/home-care-worker-immigration-pilots/employers.html)




### Home Child Care Provider and Home Support Worker pilots: Option 2 of 4

**Closed to new applicants on June 17, 2024**

You can only hire workers through these pilots if **either**

- the worker already has an open work permit without restrictions that would prevent them from working in child care or health services
- the worker already has a work permit under one of the pilots



### Temporary Foreign Worker Program: Option 3 of 4

You can only hire workers through this program if they meet these 2 conditions:

1. the worker **either**
   - already has a work or study permit
   - is allowed to work in Canada [without a work permit](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/permit/temporary/need-work-permit-work.html) (this doesn’t apply to [business visitors](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=434&top=16))
   - will work in Quebec
2. you can get a positive [Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA)](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/caregiver/apply.html) and have your candidate [apply for a new LMIA-based work permit](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/caregivers/work-temporarily-caregiver.html#new-work-permit)

Hire through the [Temporary Foreign Worker](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/caregiver.html)



### International Mobility Program (open work permits other than the pilots): Option 4 of 4

You can only hire workers under this program if

- the worker already has an **open work permit** without restrictions that would prevent them from working in child care or health services

## Hire a worker who’s outside Canada

If you’re an employer (outside Quebec), you can hire a home care worker through one of the programs below.

### Home Care Worker Immigration Pilots: Applicants not working in Canada stream: Option 1 of 4

Closed

[How to hire under this pilot](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/caregivers/home-care-worker-immigration-pilots/employers.html) (once it opens)

### Home Child Care Provider and Home Support Worker pilots: Option 2 of 4

**Closed to new applicants on June 17, 2024**

You can only hire workers through these pilots if **either**

- you already submitted a job offer to a worker, and they submitted their permanent residence application before the program closed
- the worker already has an open work permit without restrictions that would prevent them from working in child care or health services

  

### Temporary Foreign Worker Program: Option 3 of 4

You can only hire through this program if the worker will work in Quebec.

Hire through the [Temporary Foreign Worker Program](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/caregiver.html).

## Hire a worker as an employer in Quebec

If you’re an employer in Quebec, you can hire a home care worker who’s outside or in Canada through the [Temporary Foreign Worker Program](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/caregiver.html).
