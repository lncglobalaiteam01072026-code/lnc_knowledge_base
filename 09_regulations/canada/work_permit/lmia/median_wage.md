# Hire a temporary foreign worker in a high-wage or low-wage position

The wage being offered for the position will determine if you need to apply for a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) under the stream for high-wage positions or the stream for low-wage positions.

To determine which stream you need to apply under, compare the wage you’re offering for the position on your LMIA application with the [hourly wage threshold](#threshold) shown in the [Hourly wage threshold by province or territory](#table1) table below.

If the [offered wage](#offeredwage) is:

- **at or above** the provincial or territorial hourly wage threshold, you must apply under the [stream for high-wage positions](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/median-wage/high.html) and [meet the program requirements](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/median-wage/high/requirements.html) of this stream
- **below** the provincial or territorial hourly wage threshold, you must apply under the [stream for low-wage positions](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/median-wage/low.html) and [meet the program requirements](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/median-wage/low/requirements.html) of this stream

**Important:** Offering a higher wage to temporary foreign workers isn’t sufficient to qualify under the stream for high-wage positions. Wages offered to temporary foreign workers should be similar to wages paid to Canadians and permanent residents hired for the same job and work location, and with similar skills and years of experience. Adjusting the offered wage to fit a specific program stream or to avoid a program requirement could lead to a negative LMIA decision.

## Definitions

### Hourly wage threshold

The hourly wage threshold is the applicable provincial or territorial median hourly wage plus (+) 20%. The provincial or territorial hourly median wage is based on the Statistics Canada Labour Force Survey.

This wage threshold acts as the reference point against which an employer compares their offered wage to determine whether the LMIA application must be submitted under the stream for high-wage positions or the stream for low-wage positions.

### Offered wage

This is the wage rate you're offering for the position on the LMIA application. Employers must compare this wage to the applicable hourly wage threshold to determine whether the LMIA application must be submitted under the stream for high-wage positions or the stream for low-wage positions.

To meet the program's wage requirement, the offered wage must be consistent with the prevailing wage rate for the occupation.

Consult the program requirements for information on the prevailing wage:

- [Program requirements for the high-wage stream](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/median-wage/high/requirements.html#h2.10)
- [Program requirements for the low-wage stream](/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/median-wage/low/requirements.html#h2.11)
