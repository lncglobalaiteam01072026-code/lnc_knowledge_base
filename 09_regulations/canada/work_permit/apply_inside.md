# Work permit: Applying from inside Canada

---

**Not inside Canada?**

Check if you can apply from [outside Canada](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/permit-outside.html) or at a [port of entry (POE)](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/permit-poe.html).

---

Most foreign nationals need a work permit to work in Canada. You may be eligible for an employer-specific or open work permit.

![](/content/dam/ircc/images/icons/ptclock.png)
