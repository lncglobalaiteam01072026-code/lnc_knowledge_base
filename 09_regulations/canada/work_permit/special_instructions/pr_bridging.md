# Work permits for permanent residence applicants

Apply for a work permit that lets you or your family members work while you wait for a decision on your permanent residence.

## Most requested

- [Sign in to your account](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/account.html)
- [Check our current processing times](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html)
- [Check your application status](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-status.html)
- [Find a job on Job Bank](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/foreign-candidates)

## Services and information

### [Bridging open work permits (BOWPs)](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/pr-work-permits/bridging.html)

For permanent residence applicants (including Express Entry, provincial nominees) with an expiring work permit

### [Spousal sponsorship](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/family-sponsorship/spouse-partner-children/spouse-common-law-partner-canada-open-work-permit.html)

Open work permit for a sponsored spouse or common-law partner and accompanying children

### [Home Child Care Provider Pilot and Home Support Worker Pilot](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/caregivers/child-care-home-support-worker/some-experience-get-experience.html) Closed

Open work permit to meet the 12-month work experience requirement in the Gaining experience category

### [Temporary resident (TR) to permanent resident (PR) pathway](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/tr-pr-pathway/open-work-permit.html)

Open work permits for you and your family members

### [Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP)](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/atlantic-immigration/how-to-immigrate/temporary-work-permit.html)

2-year work permit to work for the employer who offered you the job

### [Start-up visa](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/start-visa/work-permits.html)

2-year open work permit to start setting up your business while you wait for a decision

### [Quebec-selected skilled workers](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/quebec-skilled-workers/international-mobility-program-plus-csq.html)

Work permits for Quebec Selection Certificate (CSQ) holders

### [Rural and Northern Immigration Pilot (RNIP)](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/rural-northern-immigration-pilot/optional-tr.html)

1-year work permit to work for the employer who offered you the job in the participating community

### [Rural Community Immigration Pilot (RCIP)](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/rural-franco-pilots/rural-immigration/work-permit.html)

2-year work permit to work for the employer who offered you the job in the participating community

### [Francophone Community Immigration Pilot (FCIP)](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/rural-franco-pilots/franco-immigration/work-permit.html)

2-year work permit to work for the employer who offered you the job in the participating community

### [Agri-Food Pilot (AFP)](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/agri-food-pilot/work-permit.html)

Open work permits for your family depending on your TEER category

### [Yukon Community Pilot](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/provincial-nominees/non-express-entry/after-apply/yukon-community-pilot.html) Closed

Location-specific work permit for Yukon nominees under the provincial nominee program.

### [Hong Kong residents](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/hong-kong-residents-permanent-residence/open-work-permit.html)

3-year open work permit to work while you wait for a decision

### [Medical doctors and certain healthcare specialists](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/special-instructions/faster-processing-doctors.html)

Faster processing for your employer-specific work permit
