# Work and Travel in Canada with  International Experience Canada

## Follow:

- [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/CitCanada/)
- [Twitter](https://twitter.com/CitImmCanada/)
- [Linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/company/citizenship-and-immigration-canada/posts/?feedView=all)

If you're 18 to 35 (18 to 30 in some countries), come work and travel in Canada through International Experience Canada (IEC)! It's a government program designed to help you

- gain valuable international work experience
- improve your language skills in English and French
- work in Canada and fund the trip of a lifetime

Are you Canadian? [Learn how you can work and travel abroad with IEC.](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadians/international-experience-canada.html)

## The 2026 IEC season is open

The pools for the 2026 season are open. If you’re eligible, you can [create a profile](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/iec/become-candidate.html).

[Check the rounds of invitations](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/work/iec/selections.asp) for key dates and available spots.

### If you’re in Canada with a valid letter of introduction (LOI)

- You may be able to get your IEC work permit mailed to a Canadian address if [you meet certain requirements.](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/iec/after-apply-next-steps.html#in-canada)
- If you don’t meet these requirements, you must leave Canada after you get your port of entry LOI and re-enter from a country [other than the US or Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon](/en/border-services-agency/news/2024/12/ending-flagpoling-for-work-and-study-permits-at-the-border.html) to activate your work permit.
