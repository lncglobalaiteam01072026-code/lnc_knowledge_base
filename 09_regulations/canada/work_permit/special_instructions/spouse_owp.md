# Open work permits for family members of foreign workers

Some family members of foreign workers in Canada may be eligible to apply for an [open work permit](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=176).

A family member is defined as a

- spouse or common-law partner
- dependent child
- dependent child of the dependent child (for example: grandchildren)

![](/content/dam/ircc/images/icons/ptclock.png)

**Processing times**

[Check processing times](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html)

![](/content/dam/ircc/images/icons/feesdollar.png)

**Fees**

Work permit fee: CA$155

Open work permit holder fee: CA$100
