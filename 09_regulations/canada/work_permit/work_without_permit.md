# Who can work without a work permit

Most foreign nationals need a work permit to work in Canada. Some workers, such as business visitors, foreign representatives and military personnel, are exempt.

## On this page

- [Check if you need a work permit](#check-permit)
- [Jobs that may not need a work permit](#no-permit)
- [Check if you need a visa to enter Canada](#check-visa)

## Check if you need a work permit

You may not need a work permit

- if your job is exempt **or**
- if you meet the requirements for the exemption

[Check if you need a work permit.](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit.html)

## Jobs that may not need a work permit

You **may not** need a work permit if you’re working in an occupation included in the list below. However, even if your occupation is listed, you may still need to meet certain requirements to be eligible for a work permit exemption.

### Academics and skilled workers

- [examiners and evaluators](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/examiner-evaluator.html)
- [public speakers](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/public-speaker.html)
- [short-term researchers](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/short-term-researcher.html)
- [short-term high-skilled workers](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/short-term-high-skill.html)

### Business people

- [business visitors](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/business/visitors-events-conferences.html)
- [convention organizers](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/convention-organizer.html)
- [public speakers](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/public-speaker.html)

### Emergency and transportation

- [aviation accident or incident investigator](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/aviation-investigator.html)
- [civil aviation inspectors](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/aviation-inspector.html)
- [crew members](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/crew-member.html)
- [emergency service providers](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/emergency.html)

### Military, foreign organizations and law

- [expert witnesses and investigators](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/witness-investigator.html)
- [foreign government representatives, diplomats and United Nations representatives](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/foreign-representative.html)
- [family members of foreign government representatives](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/foreign-representative-family.html)
- [military personnel](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/military.html)

### Sports, media and arts

- [advertising or commercial crews](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/advertising.html)
- [athletes and coaches](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/sports-non-canada.html)
- [judges and referees](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/judge-referee.html)
- [news reporters and film or media crews](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/news-film-media.html)
- [short-term performing artists](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/short-term-performing-artist.html)

### Students

- [health care students](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/healthcare-student.html) Short-term only
- [recent graduates from a Canadian designated learning institution who have applied for a work permit](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html#after)
- [full-time study permit holder working **off campus**](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html)
- [full time study permit holder working **on campus**](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-on-campus.html)

### Religious work

- [religious leaders](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit/religious-leader.html)

## Check if you need a visa to enter Canada

Even if you don’t need a work permit to work, you’ll likely still need a visitor visa or an electronic travel authorization (eTA) to enter Canada.

[Check if you need a visitor visa or an eTA](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/visit/visas.asp).
