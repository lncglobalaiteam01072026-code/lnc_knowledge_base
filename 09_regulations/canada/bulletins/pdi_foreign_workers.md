# Temporary workers

This section contains policy, procedures and guidance used by IRCC staff. It is posted on the department’s website as a courtesy to stakeholders.

A work permit or authorization to work without a permit is required in order for a foreign national to be allowed to work in Canada under either of the following programs:

- **the Temporary Foreign Worker Program**
  - Employers must obtain a [Labour Market Impact Assessment](/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#labour_market_impact_assessment) (LMIA) to hire foreign workers to fill temporary labour and skill shortages. The LMIA verifies that there is a need for a temporary worker and that no Canadians or permanent residents are available to do the job.
- **the International Mobility Program (IMP)**
  - The IMP lets employers hire temporary workers without an LMIA. Exemptions from the LMIA process are based on both of the following:
    - the broader economic, cultural or other competitive advantages for Canada
    - the reciprocal benefits enjoyed by Canadians and permanent residents
  - The IMP also allows for certain foreign nationals to work without a permit when authorized under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations.

A complete list of all instructions related to temporary residence is available.

## Admission to Canada

In addition to meeting the eligibility requirements for the temporary resident category under which they have applied, applicants will require either a [visa](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/operational-bulletins-manuals/temporary-residents/visitors.html) or an [electronic travel authorization](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/operational-bulletins-manuals/temporary-residents/eta.html) (if they are visa-exempt).
