# Labour market information data files 
The labour market information directorate offers access to information, resources, and data sets that are disclosed as part of the federal government’s commitment to enhance accountability and transparency.
The following datasets are accessible via the [Government of Canada Open Data Portal](https://open.canada.ca/en):
  * [National Occupational Classification (NOC) 2021 Version 1.0](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/1feee3b5-8068-4dbb-b361-180875837593)
  * [Occupational and Skills Information System (OaSIS)](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/10ce43bd-fb58-4969-806b-4bffebc87bec)
  * [Skills and Competencies Taxonomy (SCT)](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/618d2756-8c37-4f99-b184-8b3c1ef1b0f5)
  * [Career Handbook (CH) 2016 - Version 1.0](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/eaaf7c87-8ffe-4d68-8110-91b533da2b6f)


If you are interested in integrating labour market information directly into your Internet-enabled applications, please [contact us](https://noc.esdc.gc.ca/Home/ContactUs) for access to LMI APIs.
