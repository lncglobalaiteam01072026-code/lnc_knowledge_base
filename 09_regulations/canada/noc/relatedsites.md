#  Related sites 
## [Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada ](http://www.cic.gc.ca/english/department/index.asp)
Provides information and resources for newcomers and those wishing to immigrate to Canada about visas, immigration and citizenship, and application procedures.
## [Canadian Information Centre for International Credentials (CICIC) ](http://www.cicic.ca/2/home.canada)
Provides information for internationally-trained workers to have their credentials assessed and recognized in Canada. Contains links to various Canadian professional licensing authorities and regulatory bodies.
## [Foreign Credential Recognition (FCR) Program ](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/foreign-credential-recognition.html)
Provides information on the integration of internationally trained professionals and tradespersons into the Canadian labour market and on the recognition of credentials obtained outside of Canada.
## [Foreign Worker Program ](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers.html)
Contains information for Canadian employers who wish to hire temporary or permanent foreign workers, and for foreign workers who wish to find temporary or permanent employment in Canada.
## [Interprovincial Standards Red Seal Program ](http://www.red-seal.ca/w.2lc.4m.2-eng.html)
Contains information on national occupational standards, trade certification for all Red Seal designated trades in Canada, and provides contact information for all provincial and territorial apprenticeship and certification authorities.
## [Job Bank ](http://www.jobbank.gc.ca/home-eng.do?lang=eng)
Offers job search tips and employment resources for job-seekers and employers, as well as links to current Canadian employment opportunities.
## [Statistics Canada ](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/eng/start)
Provides statistics on the Canadian economy, such as the labour force, employment rate, and unemployment rate.
