If you want to know more about how occupations are classified within the National Occupational Classification, you can contact us using one of the three ways listed below. If your question pertains to a federal or provincial program, such as Immigration or the Temporary Foreign Worker Program or if you are seeking help to find a job or to find labour market information by occupation, please consult the [Related websites](https://noc.esdc.gc.ca/Home/RelatedSites) section.
If you are interested in integrating Labour Market Information (LMI) directly into your Internet-enabled applications, you can ask for the creation of an LMI API account.
To request an API account, please contact us and enter “API account creation” in the subject line.
