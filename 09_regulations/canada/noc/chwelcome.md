# Welcome to the Career Handbook
Please be aware that the Career Handbook has been archived as of October 31, 2024. The archival does not affect the data or regular search functions; however, the **Advanced Search** functionality is no longer available. An offline tool for the **Advanced Search** is available and can be requested by emailing NC-CNP-NOC-GD@hrsdc-rhdcc.gc.ca.
The Career Handbook is the counselling component of the National Occupational Classification (NOC) system. The Career Handbook 2016 represents the transposition of content from the Career Handbook 2003 to the structure of NOC 2016.

## Search tips
Not familiar with the NOC structure? No worries, you can search the NOC by typing in job titles using the search tab "Search by job title".
You are familiar with the NOC structure and want to access a given NOC code quickly? Search the NOC using the tab “Search by NOC code”.
Still cannot find your NOC code?
Try using job titles similar to yours in the tab “Search by job title” or else, try our advanced search tool.
